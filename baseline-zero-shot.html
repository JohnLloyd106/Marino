KNOW WHAT'S IN OUR WATERS

A guide to local fish

A few familiar species and what makes each one special.

FROM SEA
TO SHORE
◉01
PELAGIC FISH

Yellowfin tuna Tambakol

A fast-swimming open-water fish, recognized by its long yellow dorsal and anal fins. An important food and commercial species across the tropics.

↗ Open-ocean swimmer
◉02
FRESHWATER FISH

Tilapia Tilapya

A hardy freshwater fish commonly raised in ponds and inland waters. Tilapia adapts well to aquaculture and is a familiar food fish.

↗ Freshwater habitat
✳03
COASTAL CRUSTACEAN

Mud crab Alimango

Found in mangrove forests and brackish estuaries. Healthy mangroves provide important shelter and feeding grounds for mud crabs.

↗ Mangrove habitat
◉04
COASTAL & BRACKISH FISH

Milkfish Bangus

A schooling fish that lives in warm coastal waters and brackish ponds. It is widely farmed and an important food fish in the Philippines.

↗ Coastal & brackish water
